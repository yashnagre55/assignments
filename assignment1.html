WHITE ROSE

White rose one of the beautiful flower in the world..

Symbolism and Meaning White roses universally symbolize purity, innocence, and new beginnings. In Western cultures, they are a classic choice for weddings, representing fidelity, loyalty, and the start of a shared future. Conversely, in many Eastern cultures, they are associated with remembrance and spiritual passage, often used in memorial rituals to honor the deceased. Historically, the white rose also represented the House of York during the Wars of the Roses and served as a symbol of moral courage for the WWII resistance group.
